Escarpment implementation
-------------------------
`EscarpmentErosionField` is deterministic, analytic and removal-only. Four fixed coarse macro
probes estimate local relief, the face normal and a signed formation-edge distance. Candidate
columns then evaluate occupancy independently at every Y inside the existing fissure-adjusted
rock envelope.

Soft units retreat farther than the medium baseline; andesite/diorite and basalt/blackstone
survive progressively farther forward. Coherent undercuts require a resistant supported cap,
use at most 6 blocks of differential/material boundary offset around the selected face in the
source profile (16 hard runtime limit), and cannot remove the foundation layers. That offset
does not measure the broader smooth macro apron replaced by the steep face. Strong faults and
sand passes bypass the field, and shallow one- or two-block outcrops remain preserved.

The existing fissure system now exposes intersection strength from overlapping traces.
Intersections modestly strengthen/deepen the fissure. Fracture proximity/intersections and
coarse exposure to the fixed 24-degree wind increase retreat without turning every fissure into
a canyon; the fracture contribution attenuates below the fissure's design depth.

Talus
-----
Existing `lithology.talus` settings are now active. Low-side, deterministic patch gating
creates localized cliff/fissure-outlet aprons up to 7 blocks thick over an 18-block falloff.
The supplied combined-suitability threshold is 0.44. Gravel is the principal matrix; coherent
minority clasts come from the adjacent source unit. Talus begins above surviving rock and all
full dune blocks. An overlapping fractional dune layer is omitted, leaving full-block support
instead of unsupported gravel.

Backward compatibility
----------------------
`terrain.erosion` and all of its subfields decode through optional defaults. Missing erosion
data defaults to `enabled=false`, so a serialized 0.5.13 generator retains old morphology in
new chunks. The 0.5.14 source preset explicitly stores `enabled=true` and profile version 514.
Existing generated chunks are never rewritten.

  /dune geology profile
