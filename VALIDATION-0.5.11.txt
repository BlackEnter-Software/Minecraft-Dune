Foundation behavior
-------------------
The current Arrakis base layers are:

  Y 55..64 : sand
  Y 45..54 : sandstone
  Y  0..44 : stone

For a column with visible native rock, the 0.5.11 generator scans downward from Y=64 and
finds the first existing hard crust at Y=44. It then writes contiguous stone from Y=45
through the visible formation top.

A column with no visible geology skips this operation and retains the normal sand/sandstone
stratigraphy.

Limitations
-----------
The complete NeoForge Gradle build cannot be executed in this environment. Run:

  .\gradlew.bat clean build

after applying the overlay.
